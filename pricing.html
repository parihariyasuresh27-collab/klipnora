✦ Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked Questions

Have questions about Klipnora plans? Find your answers here.

How does the free trial work? +
You can export up to 5 videos for free. All free trial exports include a Klipnora watermark and are limited to 1080p quality. After using all 5 exports, you need to choose a paid plan to continue exporting videos.
What happens after my Weekly plan expires? +
Your Weekly Creator access lasts for 7 days. After expiry, you will need to purchase another plan to continue using paid features and exporting videos.
Does the Monthly plan include 4K exports? +
The Monthly Pro plan includes exports up to 1080p. 4K export is not included in the current plans. Export quality can also depend on the original video and the processing system.
How many exports do I get with Yearly Ultimate? +
Yearly Ultimate includes up to 100 video exports per month during the active subscription period. Unused monthly exports do not automatically roll over to the next month.
What is included in cloud storage? +
Yearly Ultimate includes 5 GB of cloud storage for eligible projects and files. Storage availability and file retention will follow the account's storage policy.
Can I upgrade my plan later? +
You can choose another plan when you need more features or a longer access period. The available upgrade and billing options will be shown in your account.
Will my subscription renew automatically? +
Renewal settings will depend on the payment system used by Klipnora. Before making a payment, the plan duration, price and renewal terms will be displayed clearly.
